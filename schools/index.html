DJ, Sound, Video, Photography & Recording for Schools

We work with campuses and districts on events, sound, and media. All four divisions are available under one vendor.

  • DJ Doomarz: DJ and sound reinforcement for dances, pep rallies, assemblies, and graduations, with music screened for the age group.
  • Joe Filmed It: video and photography for ceremonies, recruitment, and district communications.
  • Shot By KD0n: game-day photography and highlight video for football, basketball, soccer, and other athletics.
  • Joe's House Music Studio: audio recording for choir, band, and theater programs.
  • Custom t-shirt printing: spirit shirts, team and club shirts, and event shirts, priced by quantity with no minimum.

We have provided professional live sound and production services since 2009, working with clubs, churches, live events, and organizations throughout the Houston area. Current production work includes serving on the WORIC Media Team, operating an Allen & Heath Avantis digital console for live band, choir, praise team, speakers, and pastoral microphones, and supporting live broadcast audio across multiple streaming platforms.
