Joe's House is a full recording studio — tracking, mixing, mastering, and production. The room is acoustically treated and monitored, so what gets recorded here still sounds right when it leaves.

Sessions run from a single song to a finished project. The room also handles voiceover, podcast, and narration work. Bring a rough idea or bring stems for mixing; either way you leave with something finished.

  • Recording and tracking
  • Mixing and mastering
  • Beat and instrumental production
  • Voiceover, podcast, and narration
  • Vocal production and comping

Recent work

  • DJ services for events of any size

  • Stage and dance floor lighting
