Joe's House is a full recording studio — tracking, mixing, mastering, and production. The room is acoustically treated and monitored, so what gets recorded here still sounds right when it leaves.

For school districts

We work with campuses and districts on events, sound, and media. All three divisions are available under one vendor.

DJ and sound reinforcement for dances, pep rallies, assemblies, and graduations. Video and photography for ceremonies, athletics, recruitment, and district communications. Audio recording for choir, band, and theater programs.

We have provided professional live sound and production services since 2009, working with clubs, churches, live events, and organizations throughout the Houston area. Current production work includes serving on the WORIC Media Team, operating an Allen & Heath Avantis digital console for live band, choir, praise team, speakers, and pastoral microphones, and supporting live broadcast audio across multiple streaming platforms.
