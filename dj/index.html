DJ Doomarz

DJ, MC, Live Sound & Lighting for Schools and Events

DJ Doomarz brings the music, the microphone, and the system: DJ services, MC and hosting, sound reinforcement, and stage lighting.

School dances, pep rallies, assemblies, graduations, banquets, field days, award nights, weddings, and private parties across Rosharon, Pearland, Manvel, Alvin, and Houston. Setups scale from a single speaker and a microphone for a morning assembly up to a full system for a gym or auditorium.

  • DJ services for events of any size
  • MC and event hosting
  • Sound reinforcement and live mixing
  • Stage and dance floor lighting
  • Microphones and podium sound for speakers

For school and family events, music is screened for the age group and the program. Playlists and cues can be worked out with staff ahead of time.

Running live sound since 2009, currently on the WORIC Media Team on an Allen & Heath Avantis digital console for live band, choir, speakers, and broadcast audio.
