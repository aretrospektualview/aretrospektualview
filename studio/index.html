Joe's House Music Studio

Recording, Mixing & Mastering Studio in Rosharon, TX

Joe's House is a full recording studio: tracking, mixing, mastering, and production. The room is acoustically treated and monitored, so what gets recorded here still sounds right when it leaves.

Sessions run from a single song to a finished project. The room also handles voiceover, podcast, and narration work, and is available for school choir, band, and theater recordings. Artists come in from Pearland, Manvel, Alvin, Missouri City, Sugar Land, and across south Houston.

  • Recording and tracking
  • Mixing and mastering
  • Beat and instrumental production
  • Voiceover, podcast, and narration
  • Vocal production and comping

Studio questions

Do I need to bring an engineer?

No. Every recording session includes professional engineering.

Can you mix a song I recorded somewhere else?

Yes. External mix and master starts at $125 per song, depending on track count and the condition of the files. Two revisions are included.

Do you record podcasts and voiceover?

Yes. The room handles voiceover, podcast, and narration work as well as music.
