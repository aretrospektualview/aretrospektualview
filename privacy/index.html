Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

A Retrospektual View ("we," "us") runs aretrospektualview.com. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the site, book a service, or buy from us, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Booking details. When you book through our scheduler, we receive your name, email, phone number, the service and time you chose, and anything you write in the booking form.
  • Order details. When you buy merchandise, we receive your name, email, phone number, shipping address, and the items you ordered.
  • Messages. If you call, text, or email us, we keep that conversation so we can respond and do the work.
  • Payment information. Card and wallet payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

How we use it

We use your information to schedule and deliver the services you book, fulfill and ship orders, send receipts, order updates, and tracking numbers, answer your questions, and keep the business records the law requires. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Services that process your information

  • Acuity Scheduling runs our booking calendar.
  • Stripe processes payments, including Apple Pay.
  • Cloudflare hosts the site and may log basic technical data such as IP address and browser type for security.
  • Email delivery providers send order confirmations and shipping updates.
  • Embedded media from YouTube and Apple Music loads on some pages and is governed by those companies' own policies.

Photos and video from events

We may share photos or video from events we cover on this site and our social media. If you would like an image of you or your child taken down, contact us and we will remove it.

How long we keep it

We keep booking and order records for as long as we need them to provide the service and meet tax and accounting requirements, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, subject to records we are required to keep. Email arvmusicgroup@gmail.com or call 832-799-9733.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information online. School and family bookings are made by an adult.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date.
